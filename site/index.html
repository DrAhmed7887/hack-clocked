Evidence-Linked Clinical Handoff

When a patient arrives with records from several countries, the receiving clinician needs to know which facts are supported, which conflict, and which are missing. This tool reads every record, labels each medication, allergy and problem, quotes the exact source words, and writes a short handoff drafted by Clocked Fill and checked sentence by sentence by Clocked Verify. Sentences the checker cannot confirm are removed; absences and gaps are filled from fixed rules and labelled.

Pick a synthetic patient. Each has 2 to 4 records from Cairo, Alexandria and Valencia; Valencia GP letters are in Spanish.
